# M3 report: text and marks

Status: **M3 built: 3a text layer, 3b selection, 3c Mark tool, 3d Extract list, 3e jump and Markdown copy.** Hands-on review with the real pen is the open item (see "To try").

## What works

- **Text layer** (`domain/text/page_text.dart`, `data/pdf/page_text_service.dart`). Per page, pdfium characters and boxes become words and lines. Quotes are normalized: whitespace collapsed, line-end hyphens removed (hard hyphens and pdfium's generated soft hyphen), ligatures expanded. Cached on disk, extracted lazily in pdfrx's worker. Real pdfium: 9 ms/page on the 166-page math paper, 18 ms/page on the prose paper (Linux).
- **Selection** (`domain/text/text_selection.dart`). Nearest word within tolerance, drag extends to the nearest word across lines and columns, one even row per line, sentence selection, free rectangle fallback.
- **Mark tool** (`features/canvas/marks/`). Pen, mouse (primary button) or, with "finger draws", a finger. Live preview, release commits one `Highlight` (saved immediately, undoable, shown at once). Tap = word, double tap = sentence. Meaning comes from the tool rail.
- **Extract** (`features/extract/`). Reading-order list with meaning, page and serif quote; filters; delete with Undo; tap jumps and outlines the highlight; "Copy as Markdown" (button, phone menu, Ctrl+C) with "Copied" feedback.
- **Never lose a mark.** A highlight is written on release; leaving the screen waits for queued saves; a failed save takes the highlight back off the page.

## Acceptance (SPEC 18)

- *Drag-select matches expected text on fixture PDFs*: `test/data/text_selection_pdfium_test.dart` (abstract, a paragraph, backwards, tap, sentence, through a displayed equation) and the whole app end to end in `test/features/canvas/mark_flow_test.dart` (import, open, mouse drag, saved quote, Extract, jump, copy, delete, undo).
- *Extract matches marks exactly*: `extract_panel_test.dart`, `extract_items_test.dart`, and the flow test.
- *Export golden tests*: `test/domain/goldens/export_*.md` (re-record with `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1`).

## Found by running real PDFs

- Punctuation boxes are tiny; word splitting and line grouping must scale by the taller glyph and use the line's band, not its first word.
- Hairline glyphs (fraction bars, dashes) gave 0.5 pt highlight rows; rows are now at least 6 pt.
- Leaving the screen right after a mark could drop the save; fixed.
- The single pdfium worker serves tiles and text, so text can queue behind tile renders after a big view change. Text for the pages in view is requested when the view settles, and a mark that starts early waits and catches up. Not measured on the S9.

## Known gaps

- **Tab S9 checked only with `adb input`**, not by hand: a stylus-source drag marks, a finger swipe pans and never marks, Extract updates, tapping an item moves the canvas. With 8 multiply highlights on screen the profile overlay read build 1.1 ms, raster 3.3 ms, worst frame 10 to 16 ms (isolated spikes while swiping). Real S Pen pressure, hover, button and palm contact, and Wacom on Linux, are not exercised.
- Multi-column and math reading order follows the PDF content stream (SPEC 6.2 known limitation). Right-to-left text is not specially handled.
- A free rectangle shows as "Image region" in Extract; a page crop thumbnail (SPEC 6.2 proposal) is not built.
- Pages with a nonzero `/Rotate` use pdfrx's own rotation handling and have no test.
- Cached page text is not purged when a PDF is purged (purging itself is M5).
- Settings for highlight style (multiply / flat / pattern) and Extract order are M6.
- Hit-testing existing highlights (select, erase, tap to focus) is M4.
