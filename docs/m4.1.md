# M4.1 report: the ten fixes (F1 to F10)

Status: **built, 1,004 tests pass, analyze clean.** Waiting for your hands on the Tab S9 for F9 (palm) and F10 (pen on notes).

## What changed, by your list

| # | Your item | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Erase all | Erase panel: **Clear page ink**, **Clear all ink** (Undo snackbar, no dialog), **Clear board** (asks first, Undo after). |
| 2 | Any colour, last 5 | Picker (hue bar, square, hex). Ink and Marker each keep their **own** current colour and **own** last five, across launches. |
| 6 | No repeat highlights | Same words, same colour: nothing new (the existing one is pointed at). Same words, other colour: it changes colour. Overlapping same colour: merged into one. |
| 7 | Pen-friendly highlight menu | After marking, and on tapping a highlight with Pan or Select, a vertical strip: **Add note, Change colour, Copy, Delete**. Long-press and right-click still work. |
| 8 | Horizontal, foldable panels | One horizontal panel per tool, in one place. **Double-tap the tool** (or press its key twice) to fold it into a small tab; tap the tab to reopen. Folds itself after the first stroke (Settings switch). |
| 9 | Palm | Palms are set aside one by one and never block your fingers. Fingers far from the hovering pen work. A contact that has rested 600 ms is treated as a hand when a second finger lands. |

Also: the Extract panel now lists **colours** (chips with counts) instead of Claim, Method and Question; Markdown groups by colour in order of first use under an automatic name ("Coral", "Blue"). Old highlights keep their colour.
