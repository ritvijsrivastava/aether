# M4 report: ink, notes, links, select, undo

Status: **M4 built (4a to 4h).** Waiting for the review stop: the pen feel, palm rejection and a few things only your hands can check (below).

## What works

- **Pen pipeline** (`features/canvas/input/`). One tool router; every pointer sample reaches the active tool. Pen intent is per stroke: the eraser end, or the barrel button held at touch-down, means Erase. The button bits are a per-platform table (S Pen bit 2, Wacom bit 4). A pen that reports no pressure (X11) draws at constant width.
- **Ink.** Pressure-width ribbon, quadratic smoothing, 1 to 2 predicted points on Android, Ramer-Douglas-Peucker on release (0.15 px), saved on pen-up. Pen size is in screen dp (so it is the size you picked at any zoom); four colours. A stroke belongs to the page it starts on and may run past the edge; off every page it is canvas ink.
- **Erase.** Whole stroke by default, or the part under the eraser (switch). Wipe over strokes and highlights; marks vanish as touched; one undo step for the whole wipe; Escape puts everything back.
- **Notes.** Real text fields in the canvas, scaled with zoom, built only when in view; below 35% zoom, chips. Tap to place (drafts: an empty note leaves no trace), move by the header, resize from the corner, delete with a 48 dp control. Text is saved when typing pauses or the note loses focus.
- **Links.** Drag from a note to a highlight or back; snaps within 24 dp with a halo; a note can link many highlights; tap a line to select it and Remove link. Deleting a note or highlight takes its links in the same step; widening a word to its sentence keeps the link.
- **Select.** Loop (or Shift-drag rectangle), tap a mark, drag the selection to move ink and notes (highlights belong to their text and stay), pill with Recolor and Delete. Right-click or finger long-press opens a menu: change meaning, Add note (linked), Copy quote, Delete. Ctrl+C copies the selected quotes and notes.
- **Extract and Markdown.** Notes with their linked quotes; the Notes filter; `## Notes` in the Markdown, with golden files.
- **Keys.** H M P E N L S choose tools, 1 2 3 the meaning, Delete, Esc.
- **Undo.** Random sessions of 40 edits over every mark type retrace exactly through undo and redo (12 seeds), in storage and on the page, and no link is ever left pointing at nothing.

## Measured on the Tab S9 (profile build, `adb` input)

- A page with **10,000 ink strokes**: as paths, panning cost **47 ms of raster per frame** (about 20 fps, worst 163 ms): a failure of the 60 fps budget. A per-page bitmap cache fixed it: **raster 1.5 ms, worst 4 to 6 ms**, at 66%, 104% and 118% zoom, and while drawing a new stroke on top. (Debug entry: `--dart-define=AETHER_STRESS_INK=10000`, in memory only.)
- Selection and hit-testing on a 10,000-stroke page: touching() 0.07 ms per call (desktop VM).
- Eraser, notes and select were checked by `adb` swipes only.

## Found along the way

- A pen's barrel button (bit 4) was read as the **middle mouse button** and would have panned: fixed, with a test.
- **Real S Pen data from your own use** (the database): natural strokes run from 0 to about 0.25 pressure, median 0.12. The pressure curve assumed up to 0.6; it was retuned so natural writing spans about half to nine-tenths of the full width.
- Marks you drew and erased by hand earlier are in the database (37 of 47 are deleted: the eraser removes highlights it touches, as specified).

## Decisions made without you (change any)

- **Move is by dragging the selection**, not a pill button; the pill has Recolor and Delete. Highlights are not moved (they are anchored to text).
- **Notes let pointers through under Ink, Erase, Link and Select** so those tools can work over a note; edit a note with the Note, Mark or Pan tool.
- **Ink colours:** Warm Ink, coral, amber, blue. **Pen sizes** 1 to 8 dp, default 2.5.
- A link needs a saved note (one with words in it).

## To try (review stop, about 15 minutes)

1. **Pen feel.** Draw with the S Pen, light and hard. Does the line follow the pen without lag? Look at the overlay line "ink sample age avg ... ms" (Settings, Diagnostics, Performance overlay). **Over about 8 ms average means we need the native overlay (register R33).**
2. **Palm rejection.** Rest your palm on the screen while writing. Nothing but ink should appear.
3. **Two-finger pinch** and pan while the pen is near.
4. **Eraser end and barrel button**: S Pen button held = erase for that stroke. On Linux with the Wacom (Wayland): the eraser end, and button 1.
5. Notes: place, type (use the on-screen keyboard), drag, resize, delete.
6. Link a note to a highlight; select the line; remove it.
7. Lasso a few strokes and a note; move them; recolor; delete; Undo.
8. Right-click (Linux) or long-press (finger) on a highlight: Add note.
9. **Confirm Claim / Method / Question and their glyphs** (register R12).
