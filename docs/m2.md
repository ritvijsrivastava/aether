# M2 report: canvas engine and PDF pages

Status: **2a (engine) and 2b (PDF tiles) done. 2c (import, library, persisted view) not started.**

## What 2a delivers

A canvas of movable numbered placeholder cards (mixed Letter, A4 and landscape sizes) in one column, top to bottom, centred on a shared axis: `/doc/synthetic-30` and `/doc/synthetic-500`, reachable from the library's empty state in debug builds.

| Input | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| One finger | Pan, with fling inertia. Never marks, even with a mark tool active. |
| Two fingers | Pinch zoom about their centre, plus pan with the centroid. A lifted finger continues as a pan. |
| Mouse wheel | Scroll pans; Ctrl+wheel zooms about the pointer. |
| Trackpad (Linux) | Two-finger scroll pans; pinch zooms. |
| Mouse or pen | Pans under the Pan tool, with Space held, or with the middle button. Other tools do nothing until M3/M4. |
| Card handle | Any pointer on the "p. N" tab drags its card. The tab keeps a constant screen size at any zoom and is hidden when its card is under 90 px wide. |
| Keys | `+` and `-` zoom, Ctrl+0 fits the page nearest the centre, Ctrl+1 is 100%, Space is temporary pan. |
| Zoom pill | Zoom out, zoom in, Fit, and a live percentage, all animated. |
| Limits | Zoom 20% to 300%. Panning clamped to one screen beyond the cards. |
| Palm safety net | Touches are ignored while the pen hovers and for 250 ms after, and contacts larger than 24 px are ignored. A pen landing cancels an interrupted finger gesture. |
| Overlay | Settings, Diagnostics, "Performance overlay": fps, display refresh rate, build and raster ms, worst frame. |

## Structure

- `engine/view_state.dart`: pan and zoom math, anchored zoom, clamping, fit, interpolation.
- `engine/scene.dart`: cards in world space, row layout, culling, nearest page.
- `engine/canvas_controller.dart`: owns view and scene; implements gestures; fling and animations; card-moved callback.
- `input/input_arbiter.dart`: pure logic, events in and gesture calls out.
- `engine/painters.dart`, `engine/page_surface.dart`: grid, cards and handles painters, and the `PageSurface` seam that 2b fills with PDF tiles.
- `canvas_view.dart`: the `Listener`, keyboard and the three repaint layers. A pan or zoom repaints painters from notifiers and rebuilds no widget.

View math (8), arbiter (18), controller (13), scene and handle layout (8), canvas view gestures on the real widget (10), performance overlay (4). A 500-page visibility query runs in well under the test's 200 ms budget for 1000 queries.

## Changes after the first hands-on look

- **Layout is a column, not a row** (owner's call): pages scroll top to bottom, 80 px apart, centred. `Scene.row` became `Scene.column`.
- **The dot grid was always there but easy to miss** (in a row each page filled the canvas height). It is now stronger (alpha 0.20 to 0.28 in light, 0.16 to 0.22 in dark), visible on both sides of the column, and cheaper: one draw call instead of one per dot, and the pitch doubles when dots would be closer than 14 px so it never turns to noise. At most about 21 000 points on a 2560x1600 view, at the densest.

## Not done in 2a, deliberately

- No PDF, no tiles, no import (2b, 2c).
- Card moves are in memory only; saving them as commands is 2c.
- Pan and zoom are not saved per document yet (2c).
- Nothing consumes a mouse or pen drag under a mark tool (M3, M4).

## 2b: real PDF pages

`pdfrx` is back (2.6.6; it fetches pdfium once at build time). Pages are drawn from cached 512 px pdfium tiles.

### How it works

- **Levels of detail** (`pdf/tile_math.dart`): `sqrt(2)^n` pixels per page point, snapped so a pinch across a 41% range reuses tiles. Rounds up (never softer than the screen needs), clamps to 1/16..8 px per point, and backs off for huge pages.
- **Base tile**: the finest level where the whole page fits in one tile. Always requested first and kept alive, so there is something to show while sharper tiles render, and zoomed far out it is the picture.
- **Surface** (`pdf/pdf_page_surface.dart`): per frame it draws cached tiles at the wanted level, stands in the finest cached coarser level (scaled up) for any it does not have, crossfades a tile in over 140 ms, and asks the scheduler for the rest. A ring of tiles just outside the viewport is prefetched at lower priority.
- **Scheduler** (`pdf/tile_scheduler.dart`): at most 2 renders at once; priority bands base, visible, prefetch, nearest the viewport centre first; renders no longer wanted are canceled (pdfrx cancellation token); a failing tile is not retried in a storm.
- **Cache** (`pdf/lru_cache.dart`): LRU by bytes, budget 384 MB on Linux, 192 MB on tablets, 128 MB on phones. Tiles drawn this frame are protected from eviction, so the visible page never loses tiles to make room.
- **Renderer** (`pdf/pdfium_tile_renderer.dart`): one partial `page.render` per tile; upload to the GPU runs on engine threads, not the UI thread.
- Debug entry: `--dart-define=AETHER_DEBUG_PDF=/abs/path.pdf` adds "Open the debug PDF" to the library (stand-in for the picker until 2c). On Android push the file somewhere the app can read, e.g. `adb push file.pdf /data/local/tmp/`.
- The performance overlay now has a second line: tiles cached, MB, cache hit rate, renders and queue.

### Verified

- **Emulator (Android 16, x86_64, debug, software-ish GPU):** the MapReduce paper renders; at 213% zoom text is crisp; flick-scrolling across a page boundary shows the dot grid in the gap and tiles are sharp on settle; overlay at 213%: 121 tiles, 105 MB, hit 86%, queue idle; no errors in logcat.
- **Linux host:** real pdfium renders tiles at exactly the promised pixel sizes with real glyph pixels (integration tests); the desktop bundle includes `libpdfium.so` and starts cleanly. The PDF was not opened by hand on Linux.
- **Tests:** 288 pass. New: tile math (28), cache (11), scheduler (11), surface with real pixel checks (9: base tile fallback then sharp tiles, edge tile, cancellation, budget under a 40-page scroll), painter frame protocol (4), real pdfium (6).

### Bugs found and fixed on the way

- **The painter never called `endFrame`** (an edit silently failed to apply), so the scheduler was never told what to render and pages stayed blank. Found on the emulator via the overlay (tiles 0, queue 0). Now covered by a test of the frame protocol.
- `sqrt(2)^4` is not exactly 4, which made a 612 pt page 2449 px wide instead of 2448 and pulled in a stray tile. Scales are now `2^(lod/2)` with an epsilon on rounding.
- The debug title and page count were not showing for the PDF (another edit that silently did not apply).
- Test harness: one long real-time wait starved work started from the fake-time zone; waits now hand control back between polls. `flutter test` does not run native-asset hooks, so the pdfium tests use the library a previous `flutter build` left behind (or `PDFIUM_PATH`) and skip with a clear reason otherwise.

### Measured on the Galaxy Tab S9 (profile build, arm64, 2560x1600 landscape, 120 Hz, Impeller/Vulkan)

Driven over adb with single-finger swipes (no pinch or pen), MapReduce paper, performance overlay read after each run:

| Scenario | fps | build | raster | worst frame | tiles | memory | cache hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Idle after open (76% zoom) | n/a (nothing animating) | 5.6 ms avg incl. first frames | 5.3 ms avg | 20 ms (opening) | 20 | 13 MB | 92% |
| 12 hard flicks across 11 pages (76%) | **120 of 120 Hz** | 1.0 ms | 1.8 ms | **6 ms** | 150 | 92 MB | 98% |
| 300% zoom, 16 alternating hard swipes | 96 of 120 Hz (average over the window, includes idle frames) | 1.0 ms | 2.1 ms | **5 ms** | 245 | 172 MB | 97% |

- The display runs at **120 Hz** with no extra request needed (the overlay reports it; `dumpsys display` shows mode 1 at 120 Hz).
- Text is crisp at 300%; no blank paper was seen in the screenshots taken during and after the flicks; the render queue was idle after each run.
- **Memory at 300% was 172 MB against the 192 MB tablet cap**: close, and it grows as the user visits more levels of detail. The LRU holds it under the cap, but a lower budget or tile size should be considered if a lower-memory tablet is ever targeted.
- Caveats: swipes from `adb input` are single-pointer and quantised, so pinch gestures and pen input were not exercised; no sustained multi-minute session; the 200 and 500 page fixtures were not tried (the paper has 13 pages).

### Android file access (found on the S9)

pdfium could not open `/sdcard/Android/data/<app>/files/x.pdf` when the folder had been created by `adb shell mkdir` before the app existed (`FPDF_ERR_FILE`); it works when the app creates the folder itself. Debug and profile builds now create it at startup. This is only a test-harness concern: the real import (2c) copies picked files into app-private storage.
