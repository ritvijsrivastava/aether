# M3.1 report: follow-ups to text and marks

Status: **done.** Register items R1 to R4 closed (SPEC §18.1); one new item found (R32).

## What was done

- **R2, fixtures.** `tool/make_test_pdfs.py` generates `rotated.pdf` (7 pages: /Rotate 0, 90, 180, 270 with upright text, a CropBox not at the origin, and /Rotate 90 and 180 with text left sideways and upside down) and `scanned.pdf` (a picture of a page, no text layer) into the git-ignored `test/fixtures/generated/`.
- **R1, rotated pages.** The text-layer coordinates are right. For every page, the line's box matches the bounding box of the black pixels pdfium actually renders, within 5 pt, including the CropBox page. Words and lines are exact wherever the text reads left to right on screen. Test: `test/data/page_text_rotation_test.dart`.
- **Found: R32.** Text that reads sideways or upside down on screen is split into one-character words and one-character lines (the grouping assumes left-to-right rows). Its boxes are correct and nothing crashes, so highlights land on the right ink, but a drag selects odd words. The arXiv margin stamp is the common real case. Scheduled for M6 with column order (R20).
- **Scanned page.** No text, so the Mark tool becomes a free rectangle with the "(image region)" quote (test with the real scanned fixture).
- **R3, text behind tiles.** Measured on the Tab S9 (profile build): cold extraction 14 ms per page. No mark waited for text, including right after a zoom tap and right after a flick onto unseen pages. At most two tile renders are ever queued ahead of a text request, so no gating was added. Instead: the performance overlay now shows extraction time and any wait ("text waited N× · last X ms · max Y ms"), and text is also warmed when the Mark tool is chosen.
- **R4, speed.** Debug-mode VM, a page denser than any real one (3,000 words, 60 lines): hit-test 1.4 µs, nearest word 0.5 µs, a drag update covering the whole page 0.34 ms (budget 1 ms), whole-page selection 1.2 ms, widening to a sentence 2.2 ms; committing a mark costs the UI isolate 0.03 ms (budget 5 ms). Test: `test/domain/selection_perf_test.dart`.
